✅ VISION

To document, celebrate and promote the journeys, missions, and public contributions of 467+ Indian politicians across all tiers — empowering youth to understand leadership from the grassroots to Parliament.

🎬 Content Format

Format Description
🎙️ Podcast Interview 1-on-1 with politicians (local to national)
🎞️ Biopic Short Videos 5–10 min journey videos with visuals & voice-over
📜 Speech Highlights Viral-worthy speech reels from campaigns & rallies
🎓 Youth Q&A Panel Politicians answer students’ questions live or online
🗳️ Election Capsule Background, analysis & promises by candidate
🎭 Behind the Leader Human side — lifestyle, routines, families, inspiration
🌐 StateWise Playlist Categorized by State, Party, or District

📡 Marketing, Broadcasting & Publishing Strategy

  • YouTube Optimization: SEO titles like “[Name] | Political Journey | Rajneeti Ke Stambh”, chapters, Hindi+English subtitles, bold thumbnails.
  • Social Media Cross-Posting: Instagram reels, Facebook, X (Twitter), LinkedIn excerpts; WhatsApp broadcast groups (rural push).
  • PR Strategy: Tie-up with local newspapers & regional TV; local cable ads / FM interviews of guests.
  • Political Partnerships: Each featured leader shares their episode via their followers; tagging & quoting official handles.
  • Collaborations: Journalist panelists; Political science faculties; news YouTubers (e.g., Deshbhakt, Lallantop tie-ins).
