🌍 Event Concept

The International Kathak Festival in Udaipur is a prestigious 2-day cultural extravaganza celebrating the richness of Indian classical dance – Kathak – with a global touch...

🎯 Objectives

  • To position Udaipur as an international cultural destination.
  • To promote Indian classical arts and global cultural collaboration.
  • To empower artists and build intercultural partnerships.
  • To offer high-value visibility for CSR, tourism, and luxury brands.

🌟 Festival Highlights

  • Grand 2-day showcase with 3-day rehearsals & cultural exchange.
  • 12 countries participating: India, France, USA, Japan, Germany, UAE, UK, Canada, Russia, Sri Lanka, Nepal, Bangladesh.
  • 4 major themed performances + workshops + international symposium.
  • 500+ curated audience including dignitaries, diplomats, and art patrons.

🛠️ Event Planning & Management

  • Duration: 2 Main Days + 1 Rehearsal/Workshop Day.
  • Dates: Proposed between November–December 2025.
  • Venue: Udaipur (City Palace / Shilpgram / Luxury Hotel).
  • Team: 15 Artists, 5 Coordinators, 12 Country Delegates, 40 Crew Members.
  • Support: Local Cultural Delegation, Tourism Boards, Embassy Invitees.
